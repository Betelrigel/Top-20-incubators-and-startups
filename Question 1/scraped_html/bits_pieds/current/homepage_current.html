Welcome To

Pilani Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Society

Pilani Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Society (PIEDS) is an autonomous not for profit society registered in 2013 under The Rajasthan Societies Registration Act, 1958. 

PIEDS nurtures innovation-driven startups by providing incubation support, mentorship, access to investors, technical expertise, and networking opportunities. With the strong academic and research ecosystem of BITS Pilani, it serves as a hub for fostering entrepreneurship, empowering students, alumni, and external entrepreneurs to transform ideas into scalable ventures.

Explore Our Flagship Schemes

At PIEDS, BITS Pilani, we design flagship schemes that nurture entrepreneurs across every stage of their journey. These programs combine structured mentorship, funding support, and growth opportunities with the implementation of key government schemes. By integrating institutional expertise with national initiatives, we ensure startups gain the right resources to scale and succeed.

IDFC FIRST Bank IGNITE

An accelerator program supporting revenue-generating startups with scalable solutions that create measurable social and environmental.

TIDE 2.0

PIEDS TIDE 2.0 scheme offers up to ₹7 lakh funding and incubation for early-stage startups in AI, IoT, Blockchain, Robotics, AR/VR, and Fintech.

NIDHI EIR

NIDHI-EIR program at PIEDS offers up to ₹3.6 lakh fellowships, mentorship, and support for innovators to transform research ideas into successful startups.

NIDHI SSS

The NIDHI Seed Support System supports Indian startups with up to ₹1 crore equity funding, expert mentorship, and access to the BITS Pilani startup ecosystem to accelerate product trials, market entry, and commercialization.

NIDHI PRAYAS

NIDHI PRAYAS at PIEDS offers up to ₹10 lakh grants, mentorship, lab access, and IP support for early-stage hardware startups in emerging technologies.

Genesis

GENESIS EiR at PIEDS supports Tier II/III founders with up to ₹10 lakh funding, mentorship, and incubation to build and validate technology-driven startup ideas.

Samridh

SAMRIDH Accelerator by MeitY offers up to ₹40 lakh investment, mentorship, and growth support to scale tech startups in AI/ML, IoT, Blockchain, and emerging technologies.

SISFS

Startup India Seed Fund at PIEDS offers up to ₹50 lakh funding for prototype development, proof-of-concept, market entry, and commercialization of innovative startup solutions.

Samvardhan

Samvardhan is a 6-month healthcare hardware accelerator offering ₹10 lakh funding, design mentorship, and BIRAC-backed support to scale prototypes into market-ready products.

Sustainability Accelerator

PIEDS x Susmafia Sustainability Accelerator is a 6-month program offering ₹10 lakh funding, mentorship, and industry access for early-stage climate and sustainability startups.

Womenpreneur For Bharat

Womenpreneurs for Bharat empowers women-led startups with up to ₹15 lakh funding, mentorship, incubation, and strategic support to drive inclusive innovation in smaller Indian cities.

Saksham

Saksham supports women-led startups with mentorship, incubation, infrastructure, and grants up to ₹10 lakh to foster entrepreneurship and innovation in smaller Indian regions.

Navonmesh Protsahan Spardha

MSDE and PIEDS launched Navonmesh Protsahan Spardha to convert NSTI skills into startups through mentorship.

Srishti

Hardware Prototyping EiR at BITS Pilani offers grants, mentorship, labs, and funding support for innovators building solutions in robotics, EVs, renewable energy, and related sectors.

Alumni Startup Seed Fund

Funding support of ₹5–10 lakhs available for BITSian founders to help build, launch, and scale innovative startup ideas into successful ventures.

Mookerji Innovation Fund

The Mookerji Innovation Fund, managed by Pilani Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Society, offers ₹5–50 lakh equity funding and mentorship from Silicon Road Ventures for startups with at least one BITSian co-founder.

Smt. Rajkumari Jain Seed Fund for Innovation

Rajkumari Jain Seed Fund supports BITS Pilani startups with up to ₹50 lakh funding for innovation sectors.
