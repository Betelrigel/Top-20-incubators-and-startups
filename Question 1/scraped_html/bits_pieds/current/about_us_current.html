About PIEDS

Welcome to PIEDS

Welcome to the Pilani Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Society (PIEDS) at BITS Pilani! We are a vibrant community dedicated to fostering innovation and entrepreneurship among students. Our mission is to empower aspiring entrepreneurs with the resources, mentorship, and support they need to turn their ideas into successful ventures.

Fueling the Next Generation of Innovators

Pilani Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Society (PIEDS) is the Technology Business Incubator (TBI) of BITS Pilani, established to foster a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students, alumni, and researchers. As a not-for-profit society, PIEDS provides structured incubation and pre-incubation support through funding, mentorship, technical infrastructure, and market access. With a vibrant startup ecosystem and strong institutional legacy, PIEDS continues to empower aspiring entrepreneurs to build scalable, technology-driven solutions for real-world challenges.

Our Vision

At PIEDS, we are dedicated to nurturing the entrepreneurial spirit within the BITS Pilani community. Established with the vision to foster innovation and support aspiring entrepreneurs, PIEDS provides a dynamic platform for students to transform their ideas into impactful ventures.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower students through a range of programs, including startup incubation, mentorship, workshops, and networking events. We believe in the power of collaboration and creativity, encouraging our members to take risks, think critically, and develop solutions to real-world challenges.
